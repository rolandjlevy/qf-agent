The quoting bottleneck

## The job often goes to whoever quotes first.

A clear quote on the customer's phone the same day says you're organised before you've even started.

### Leads go cold

Customers usually ask more than one trade. A quote that turns up next week turns up too late.

### Evenings disappear

Typing up quotes after a day on the tools eats the time you have left with your family.

### Things get missed

Leave out an exclusion or an assumption and it comes back as an argument on site.

How it works

Why trust it

## Built to protect your reputation.

The reason your customers trust your quote is the reason you can trust QuoteFetch: it never pretends to know something it doesn't.

Every material comes through as "Price TBC". You look up the real price and pick the one you'd actually buy — so every figure is one you chose.

FAQ

## Questions tradespeople ask.

Does QuoteFetch make up prices?

No. Every material is left as "Price TBC" until you look it up. From the saved quote you can search current prices from Screwfix, Toolstation, B&Q and Amazon and pick the one you want, so every price is one you chose.

Will it say my work meets regulations?

No. It never writes compliance claims like Part P or Gas Safe on your behalf. If you add your own certifications to your profile, it states those exactly as you wrote them.

Which trades does it work for?

Twenty UK trades, from plumbers, electricians and gas engineers to roofers, landscapers and tree surgeons. Each one gets its own questions about the job.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It's built phone-first: describe the job, take photos with your camera and copy the finished quote, all from the van.

Can I change the quote before I send it?

Always. You choose the materials before anything is written, and the finished quote is plain text — so you can change any wording before you send it.
